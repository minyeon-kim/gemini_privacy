# Gemini Privacy Revamp — prototype

A self-initiated concept that redesigns how Gemini shows and controls the personal information behind an answer. It was built for usability testing. **This is not a Google product and is not affiliated with Google.** Everything shown on the screens is fictional sample data.

- **Try it:** [minyeon-kim.github.io/gemini_privacy/?mode=participant](https://minyeon-kim.github.io/gemini_privacy/?mode=participant) (add `&lang=en` for English)
- **Case study:** [minyeon-kim.github.io/portfolio/work/gemini-privacy/](https://minyeon-kim.github.io/portfolio/work/gemini-privacy/)

## What it tests

- **Flow A** — open the sources under an answer and turn off or delete the personal information it used, right there.
- **Flow B** — ask in the chat to clear something, and see what was deleted and what was left.

## Data

During a test session the prototype records taps and survey answers under a participant code, never a name. Only sessions opened from a participant link (with a code) are saved; opening the prototype without one saves nothing to the study database. Outside test sessions the study database is closed, so nothing is stored; a visitor's own browser may keep a local copy of their session.

The Gemini sparkle is the official symbol used on [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/), shown because this is a revamp concept of that product.
